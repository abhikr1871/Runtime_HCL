## Where the data came from
- **Base catalogue:** DummyJSON demo products (https://dummyjson.com/products, MIT-licensed project): 194 downloaded, 10 cars and motorcycles excluded (they don't fit a shop assistant, and the brief's "electric cars → not found" example needs them absent), 184 kept. Prices converted from USD at a fixed demo rate of ₹83/USD; reviewer names and emails dropped. DummyJSON weights have no unit, so they stay "not listed" after normalisation.
- **Generated products:** N India-focused products generated by _model_ with `prompts/products.md`. Prices, stock and IDs set by code.
- **Shoppers and orders:** N synthetic shoppers generated by _model_ with `prompts/shoppers.md`; order product IDs picked by code.
- No real customer data, names, phone numbers or emails.

## What the fields mean
See `api/app/schemas.py` (`Product`, `Review`) and `api/app/store/schema.sql`. `weight_raw` is the value as listed; `weight_g` is the normalised value (empty when the unit is missing).
